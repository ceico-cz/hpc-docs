# Phoebe: gpu1 and gpu2 time limit raised to 14 days 4 hours

On 21 September 2026 the time limit of the `gpu1` and `gpu2` partitions was raised in two
steps: from 8 days 8 h to 10 days, then to 14 days 4 h.

<!-- more -->

The `gpu` partition (both GPU nodes) keeps its limit of 18 days 8 h, and `gpu_int` 20 days
10 h. See [GPU jobs](../../../slurm/gpu-jobs.md) for choosing a partition.
