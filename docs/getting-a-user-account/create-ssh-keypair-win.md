# Create an SSH key pair in the MobaXterm console

1. click on <kbd>Tools</kbd> and then <kbd>MobaKeyGen</kbd>
1. click on <kbd>Generate</kbd> (and move your mouse over the blank area to generate some entropy)
1. the key will be generated
1. copy out the "Public key for pasting into OpenSSH server" and provide it to Phoebe admin (eg. via mail or slack)
1. type secure passphrase into field <kbd>Key passphrase</kbd> and then confirm passphrase.
1. click on <kbd>Save private key</kbd> and save it to some appropriate location (eg. your home directory)
