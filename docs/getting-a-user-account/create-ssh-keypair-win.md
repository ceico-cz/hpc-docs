# Create an SSH key pair in the MobaXterm console

1. Click **Tools** → **MobaKeyGen**.
1. Click **Generate** and move your mouse over the blank area to generate some entropy.
1. Copy the **Public key for pasting into OpenSSH server** and send it to the CEICO HPC administrator (e.g. by e-mail or Slack).
1. Type a strong passphrase into **Key passphrase** and repeat it in **Confirm passphrase**.
1. Click **Save private key** and save it somewhere safe (e.g. your home directory).
